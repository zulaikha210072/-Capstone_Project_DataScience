# Final Project Proposal

## The Impact of Refugee Status on Access to Education: A Comparative Analysis of Afghanistan, Bangladesh, and Myanmar

##  Project Idea

For my final project, I want to explore how refugee status affects access to education in different countries. 
I will focus on three countries: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, and Myanmar, because they are strongly affected by displacement and refugee crises.
The main idea of this project is to understand whether countries with higher refugee populations face more challenges in providing access to education,
and how factors like internet access and school enrollment are affected.

## Research Question

How does refugee status influence access to education in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, and Myanmar?

## Data Sources

For this project, I will use two main sources of data:

* World Bank Open Data (education indicators)

  * School enrollment (secondary and tertiary)
  * Internet access (% of population)
  * Literacy rates (if needed)

* UNHCR Refugee Data Portal

  * Refugee population by country of origin
  * Refugee population in host countries
  * Refugee-related education statistics (if available)

## 🧹 Planned Methodology

I will:

* Collect and clean datasets from World Bank and UNHCR
* Focus only on Afghanistan, Bangladesh, and Myanmar
* Merge datasets using country and year
* Analyze relationships between refugee population and education access
* Create visualizations (bar charts, comparisons, and trends)

## 📈 Expected Outcome

I expect to find that countries with higher refugee populations may face more pressure on education systems, leading to lower access to education and digital learning opportunities.

The final goal is to show a clear connection between displacement and educational inequality.

## 🎯 Why This Project

I chose this topic because education is a very important issue for refugees, and many people around the world do not have equal access to learning opportunities. I want to use data to better understand this problem and highlight possible areas for improvement.
